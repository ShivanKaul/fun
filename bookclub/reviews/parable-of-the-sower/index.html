Shivan's review

Parable of the Sower

A reaction to Parable of the Sower by Octavia E. Butler.

Too much sexual violence especially at the beginning. It's true for a lot of Octavia Butler books. Consent and sexual violence pop up very often so I should've known better. The other one I've read is Bloodchild.

I thought the narrator was very believable and the writing in general was engaging. It was very cohesive: I've discovered over the course of the book club that I care deeply about how believable the writing is, and never once was I like huh that's weird phrasing or anything.

I hope that when the apocalypse comes we will choose each other. I feel like that's generally what happened with the pandemic. Building walls clearly never works.

I also liked how it was complicated. I think the audience is supposed to empathize with Lauren and her family but they are also building gated communities.

I actually really enjoyed the religion. It struck me as plausible. People are losing faith and hope and they need a God that justifies their current world. And I also liked the totally whacko "go to the stars" end goal, makes sense to me that that's what some people would find useful. This is exactly the credo of many tech people. The hail Mary fundamental solution to all of their problems. I also like how unintuitive it is.

There was a lot to chew on in the book.

The hyper empathy is interesting. Society has collapsed and there's multiple different responses to that, especially from people who want to lead. One response is to build wall. Another is to preach faith. Another is to say that it's the fault of outsiders. Lauren is rejecting all of those and that's probably in large part because of her hyper empathy. Violence has a real cost. But she's also not naive, she knows that she can't just turn the other cheek. So she develops this philosophy. I also thought that's what makes her interesting.

It again reminded me of Lincoln in the Bardo. Accepting the pain and suffering of people can make you act more violently to end it quickly.
