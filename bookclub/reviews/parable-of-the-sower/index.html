Depressing but engaging read

A short reaction to Parable of the Sower by Octavia E. Butler.

Depressing but engaging read.

Parable of the Sower is bleak, violent, and uncomfortably plausible, but it is also propulsive enough that the darkness does not make it feel inert.

The book works because Butler keeps the dystopia grounded in social breakdown, scarcity, migration, privatization, religion, and survival rather than turning it into abstract worldbuilding.
